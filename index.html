What we believe

    No tracking, no dark patterns, no fine print. Our software does what it says, and nothing sneaky besides.

  • Your work stays yours

    Our tools keep your work in plain files, like Markdown and JSON, that you can open, move, and back up without asking us.

  • Open foundations

    The building blocks are open source on GitHub. Read them, learn from them, or build something of your own.

  • Made to be enjoyed

    Useful is the floor, not the ceiling. We sweat the details so our software is a pleasure to use.

Platform

CLI, SDKs & APIs · For organizations Early preview

Build your own AI workflows and apps on Gezel.

The engine behind the Gezel app is open for you to build on. Drive it from the command line, connect your own software through typed SDKs and an HTTP API, and package workflows as apps your team can install. It's free, open source, and can run AI privately on your own machines.

  • A headless gezel command line for servers and scripts
  • SDKs that bring Gezel's local models, model setup, and knowledge into your own apps. DocBlocks uses them for its AI features.
  • Package roles, craftbooks, pages, and data as Gezel apps your team can install and share

Document format · TypeScript & React

Squiggly Square squisq

A small extension to Markdown: tag a heading with {[ ]} and it becomes a slide, photo grid, pull quote, map, or chart. Squisq parses, renders, and converts those documents to DOCX, PDF, EPUB, PPTX, and more, and it powers the editor inside DocBlocks.

Visit Squiggly Square GitHub squisq react editor

3D experience engine · TypeScript & three.js

Molen

An AI-legible engine for games, visualizations, and interactive 3D experiences: a deterministic headless kernel, a three.js client, and a dev loop an agent can drive end to end. Validate, simulate, assert, and screenshot entirely through text, on the CLI or over MCP.

Visit molen.dev Play the samples GitHub molen-tooling

We also publish gezk, an open format for packaging reference documents with full-text and vector indexes in a single portable file.
