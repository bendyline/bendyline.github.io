Privacy Statement

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This privacy statement applies to Bendyline's products, including Qualla and DocBlocks. We take a privacy-first approach: our products are built as static experiences that do not track you or collect your personal information.

Qualla

Qualla is a static website. We do not use cookies, per-session analytics, tracking, or user accounts. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your preferences (like map position) are stored locally in your browser and are never transmitted to us.

DocBlocks

DocBlocks is a static website and open-source editor. We do not use cookies, per-session analytics, tracking, or user accounts. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The documents you create and your preferences are stored locally in your browser and are never transmitted to us.

Server Logs

Standard web server logs, which capture information around your page requests, may be collected by our hosting provider for operational purposes (e.g., error monitoring). These logs are not used for tracking individual users.

Contact

Questions or concerns? For Qualla, visit the Qualla GitHub repository; for DocBlocks, visit the DocBlocks GitHub repository. You can also reach us at support@bendyline.com.
